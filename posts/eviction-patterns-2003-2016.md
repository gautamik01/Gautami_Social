# LinkedIn draft: Did Philly's eviction pattern change from 2003 to 2016?

Link: https://gautamik01.github.io/Gautami_Social/portals/eviction-patterns-2003-2016.html

---

Philadelphia filed about the same number of evictions in 2016 as it did in 2003. Somewhere between 9,800 and 11,200 every single year.

So I asked a different question: did the places change?

Mostly no. 72% of the neighborhoods that topped the eviction list in 2003 to 2005 were still there in 2014 to 2016. But underneath a flat citywide total, 100 neighborhoods saw evictions rise by more than a quarter and 92 saw them fall just as much.

Two maps, one city, fourteen years. Hover a neighborhood and watch it on both.
