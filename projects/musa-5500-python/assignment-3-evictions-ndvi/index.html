Data. Eviction counts by census tract come from the Eviction Lab at Princeton (2003 to 2016). Code violations come from the Philadelphia Department of Licenses and Inspections (2012 to 2016). The 15 violation types most tied to housing condition were kept, which is 34,108 violations. Each one was assigned to the tract it falls in. Poverty rate and renter share are from the 2016 Eviction Lab file.

Read with care. These are raw counts, so large tracts naturally have more of both. A strong correlation does not show that one causes the other. The violation file has no dates, so it cannot be split by year. Violation names are simplified from the city's codes.
